Exception-to-Resolution Operating System

A practical workflow designed to keep normal operations moving while making exceptions visible, owned and actionable.

1. Detect

Surface order, payment, supplier, delivery or customer exceptions early.

2. Assign

Record owner, status, blocker, next action and follow-up date.

3. Resolve / Escalate

Complete the safe action or package evidence for the decision owner.

SOP structure used

  1. Confirm the current order/account state.
  2. Identify the discrepancy and accountable owner.
  3. Gather the supporting evidence.
  4. Take the action within authority or escalate with context.
  5. Document the resolution, follow-up date and final status.

This structure reflects the way I have handled high-volume order, billing, payment and service exceptions using trackers, SOPs, templates, case notes and escalation rules.
